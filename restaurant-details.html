Deccan - Luxury Dining

Experience the elegance of Deccan - Luxury Dining, a restaurant designed for a premium culinary experience. With a sophisticated ambiance and meticulously crafted interiors, this space offers the perfect blend of comfort and luxury.

Deccan features a spacious 120-seat capacity, exclusive private dining options, and a well-planned layout covering 350m² over two floors. The restaurant also provides ample parking with 50 designated spots, ensuring a seamless experience for guests.

Designed with modern aesthetics and functionality in mind, Deccan is the perfect choice for fine dining enthusiasts and those seeking an unforgettable gastronomic journey.
